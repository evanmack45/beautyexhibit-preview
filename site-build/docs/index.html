    About the images

    Every image here is out of copyright. 13 are released as CC0 by the Rijksmuseum, and 29 are public domain because of their age. Each one has a single printed or catalogued year on its Wikimedia Commons page, and that year is the answer. We cropped out printed dates so the picture does not give it away.

    A score is 100 for the exact year, minus 3 for every year you are off. Your guesses stay in this page. There are no accounts, cookies or trackers. The challenge link holds the round number and your score, nothing else.
